01 — Introduction

Who I Am

"I turn complex electronics into clean, manufacturable circuit boards — from first schematic to production-ready Gerbers."

With deep expertise in KiCad and hands-on experience across power electronics, IoT, and embedded systems, I design PCBs that don't just work on screen — they work in production. Every trace, clearance, and via is placed with purpose and precision.

From compact sensor breakouts to industrial relay controllers and USB-C power management circuits, I bring clarity to every layer of the design. All deliverables are ready to send directly to JLCPCB, PCBWay, or any fab house — no revisions needed.

Available worldwide via Fiverr for freelance projects. Fast turnaround, clean communication, professional output.
